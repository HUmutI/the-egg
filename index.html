02 Why bother

Ankara has almost six million people. Somehow you’re still building alone at 2am.

The interesting people are out there — the engineer with forty side projects, the designer who can’t stop tinkering, the researcher, the student, the friend who keeps soldering things. They’re just stuck in the wrong rooms:

  • Panels.
  • Lanyards.
  • Mixers with name stickers.
  • “Let’s circle back.”
  • Pitch-deck karaoke.

so we made a better room ↓

One long table. Too much pizza. A deadline at midnight.

03 A day at the club

Noon to midnight,
roughly.

  1. 12:00

    Doors

    Coffee, introductions, a wall of sticky notes. Name tags optional. Opinions mandatory.

  2. 12:30

    Crack

    Anyone can pitch an idea in sixty seconds. Half-baked is the house style.

  3. 13:15

    Nest

    Teams form around whatever sounds most fun. Strangers become co-founders of a very silly thing.

  4. 13:30

    Incubate

    Heads down. Build it, break it, build it again. Mentors wander around with snacks and opinions.

  5. 18:00

    Pizza

    Non-negotiable. Laptops closed, twelve boxes open, one ongoing debate about pineapple. The second, better idea usually shows up here.

  6. 21:30

    Hatch

    Five-minute demos, everybody claps. Working is encouraged. Ambitious and broken also counts.

  7. 23:00

    Afterglow

    Cold pizza, the last metro home, and a group chat that keeps going all week.

04 Who shows up

A different mix every Sunday.

You don’t need to be a founder, an engineer, or even sure what you want to make. You just need to be curious and a little impatient.
