01 What is this

The Egg Club is a Sunday ritual for people who’d rather make something than talk about making something.

keep scrolling

02 Who comes

A different mix every time.

You don’t need to be a founder, an engineer, or even sure what you want to make. You just need to be curious and a little impatient.

— everyone has a reason to be here

Ankara has almost six million people. Somehow you’re still building alone at 2am.

03 A day at the club

Morning to evening,
roughly.

One egg, 10:30 → 18:30. Not to scale.

  1. 10:30

    Doors

    Coffee, introductions, a wall of sticky notes. Name tags optional. Opinions mandatory.

  2. 11:00

    Crack

    Anyone can pitch an idea in sixty seconds. Half-baked is the house style.

  3. 11:30

    Nest

    Teams form around whatever sounds most fun. Strangers become co-founders of a very silly thing.

  4. 12:00

    Incubate

    Heads down. Build it, break it, build it again. Mentors wander around with snacks and opinions.

  5. 16:30

    Pizza

    Non-negotiable. Laptops closed, twelve boxes open, one ongoing debate about pineapple. The second, better idea usually shows up here.

  6. 17:30

    Hatch

    Hacking stops. Five-minute pitches, everybody claps. Working is encouraged. Ambitious and broken also counts.

  7. 18:30

    Afterglow

    Lights out at 18:30. Cold pizza, the metro home, and a group chat that keeps going all week.

05 Good questions

Before you ask.

Something else? Email theeggclubb@gmail.com.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ticket, no fee, no catch. The pizza’s on us too.

Who can come?

Anyone curious who wants to make something. Students from any university (ODTÜ, Bilkent, Hacettepe…), people with jobs and people between them, designers, developers, hardware tinkerers, researchers, musicians. You don’t have to be from ODTÜ, and you don’t have to have built anything before.

Do I need a team, an idea or skills?

No, no and no. At 11:00 anyone can pitch an idea in sixty seconds, and by 11:30 teams form around the ones that sound most fun. Bring an idea if you have one; join someone else’s if you don’t. First-timers are welcome — just come to make something, not to watch.

What should I bring?

A laptop and its charger. An idea, or none. Whatever your project needs — a breadboard, a sketchbook, a guitar. Bring a power strip and you’re instantly everyone’s favourite.

Where exactly?

On the ODTÜ (METU) campus in Ankara. The exact room — and anything you need to know about getting onto campus — comes with your confirmation.

Turkish or English?

Both. Pitch, build and demo in whichever you’re comfortable with. Someone will always translate the jokes.

What about food?

Sandwiches, pizzas and snacks are on us. Vegetarian, vegan, allergic, or eating something specific? Email theeggclubb@gmail.com before Sunday and we’ll do our best — and bring a snack of your own, just in case.

Can I come for part of the day?

Life happens, so yes. But ideas get pitched at 11:00, teams form by 11:30, and everyone shows what they built at 17:30. Try to catch both ends.

How do you pick who comes?

We read every application ourselves and aim for a good mix of people. If it’s not this Sunday, there’s another one two weeks later.

Can I bring a friend?

Please do. Everyone applies on their own, so send them the link — there’s a button for that just below.

How do I hear about the next one?

Every date goes up on app.the-egg.club, and the next one shows up on this page too. Come back two Sundays from now and there’ll be a seat to apply for.
